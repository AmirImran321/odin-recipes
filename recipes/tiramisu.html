Tiramisu

A classic Italian dessert made with layers of coffee-soaked ladyfingers and creamy cheese.

Ingredients:

  • 6 large egg yolks
  • ¾ cup white sugar
  • 1 ¼ cups heavy cream
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 pound mascarpone cheese, at room temperature
  • ¼ cup strong brewed coffee, at room temperature
  • 2 tablespoons rum
  • 2 (3 ounce) packages ladyfinger cookies
  • 1 tablespoon unsweetened cocoa powder

Steps:

  1. Gather the ingredients.
  2. Whisk egg yolks and sugar together in a medium saucepan until well blended.
  3. Whisk in milk and cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until mixture comes to a boil.
  4. Remove from heat and let cool for 5 minutes.
  5. Stir in the mascarpone cheese until smooth.
  6. In a shallow dish, mix the coffee and rum.
  7. Quickly dip each ladyfinger into the coffee mixture, then layer them in a greased dish.
  8. Sift the cocoa powder over the top.
  9. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or overnight.
