Mango Lassi

A refreshing Indian yogurt drink made with ripe mangoes and a hint of cardamom.

Ingredients:

  • 2 ripe mangoes, peeled and diced
  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • ½ cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons white sugar
  • ½ teaspoon ground cardamom
  • 1 tablespoon honey (optional)

Steps:

  1. Place the diced mangoes in a blender.
  2. Add the Greek yogurt, milk, sugar, and cardamom.
  3. Blend until smooth and well combined.
  4. If desired, add honey for extra sweetness.
  5. Chill in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes before serving.
