Homemade Pizza

A classic homemade pizza with a crispy crust and your favorite toppings.

Ingredients:

  • 1 pizza dough
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Your favorite toppings (pepperoni, mushrooms, peppers, etc.)

Steps:

  1. Preheat your oven to 475°F (245°C).
  2. Roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface.
  3. Transfer the dough to a baking sheet or pizza stone.
  4. Spread the pizza sauce evenly over the dough.
  5. Sprinkle the mozzarella cheese on top.
  6. Add your desired toppings.
  7. Bake for 12-15 minutes, or until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly.
